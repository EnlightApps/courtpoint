How do I score a point?

  • Apple Watch: swipe up when you win the point, swipe down when they win it.
  • Wear OS: tap the half of the screen marked ME when you win the point, and the half marked THEM when they win it. The server is always shown on top, so the halves swap when the serve changes.

How do I undo a point?

Swipe left: the screen asks "UNDO?", and a second swipe left within two seconds undoes the point. On Wear OS, turning the crown works the same way. You can go back up to 64 steps.

Where is the menu?

Press and hold the match screen. The menu has Switch server, Lock screen, Haptics, Adjust score, How to play, End match and Reset match (End game and Reset game in pickleball).

How do I lock the screen?

Double tap the match screen, or choose Lock screen in the menu. Double tap again to unlock.

Will my match be saved if the app closes?

Yes. The match is saved after every point and picks up where you left off when you open CourtPoint again.

Which sports and formats are supported?

  • Tennis and padel: best of 1, 3 or 5 sets, tiebreaks, optional match tiebreak, No-Ad (Golden Point in padel) and doubles serve order.
  • Pickleball: games to 11, 15 or 21, side-out or rally scoring, and doubles with server 1 and 2.

Does it need my phone or an internet connection?

No. Once installed, CourtPoint scores matches, saves them and shows your history entirely on the watch. Only two buttons need an internet connection, and only when you tap them: RATE COURTPOINT, which opens the App Store or Google Play, and OPEN on the PRIVACY POLICY screen, which opens the privacy policy (on Wear OS, in your paired phone's browser).

Is there a free trial, subscription or in-app purchase?

No. CourtPoint is a one-time purchase from the App Store (Google Play: once the Wear OS app is released), with every feature included. You can download it again for free with the same store account. A purchase on one platform does not include the other.

How do I get a refund?

App Store: Apple handles refunds, so ask Apple. Google Play: you can ask Google, or email me and I can refund the order myself.

What data does CourtPoint collect?

The apps collect none. Scores, match history and settings are saved on your watch, and the apps send nothing to us or anyone else. On Apple Watch, your own iPhone and iCloud backups can include the app's settings. The details are in the Privacy Policy.
