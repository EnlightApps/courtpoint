A Turkish translation is available; if the two versions differ, this English version prevails.

2. Summary

  • The apps keep everything on your watch: scores, match times, match history and settings. None of it is sent to us or to anyone else.
  • We have no server, no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs.
  • CourtPoint is a paid app on both platforms, sold by Apple (App Store) and Google (Google Play) before you install it. The apps contain no purchase or billing code and send nothing.
  • The only personal data we receive is what you send us by email.

3. Data stored on your watch

The apps store the following only on your watch, using the platform's standard on-device storage (UserDefaults on Apple Watch; DataStore and SharedPreferences on Wear OS). Other apps cannot access it. The apps do not send it anywhere, and we never receive it. It is deleted when you delete the app, and you can delete history entries yourself at any time.

Match settings

Your setup choices, so the next match starts the same way:

  • Sport last selected: tennis, padel, or pickleball (Wear OS)
  • Sets: best of 1, best of 3, or best of 5
  • No-Ad / Golden Point scoring: on or off
  • Match tiebreak: on or off
  • Doubles: on or off
  • Pickleball game target: 11, 15, or 21 points
  • Pickleball rally scoring: on or off
  • Who serves first: you or your opponent
  • Classic court labels (D/A instead of R/L): on or off

Match in progress

Saved after every point so the match can resume if the app closes, and deleted when the match finishes, is ended or is reset:

  • Sport
  • Tennis and padel: points for each side; games for each side in each set; sets won by each side; current set number; whether a tiebreak or match tiebreak is in progress; tiebreak points for each side; who served first in the tiebreak; set format; No-Ad / Golden Point setting; match tiebreak setting; current server; who served first in the match; doubles setting; doubles serving-order position, including at the start of the tiebreak; whether the match is over and who won
  • Pickleball: score for each side; current server; server number (1 or 2); who served first; doubles setting; rally scoring setting; game target; whether the game is over and who won
  • Time played so far, as a number of seconds (no date or time of day)

Match history

Results of up to 200 completed matches, for your own statistics. Each entry contains:

  • A random identifier created on the watch, used only to select or delete the entry (it is not linked to you or your device)
  • Sport
  • Whether you won or lost
  • Match duration in seconds (no date or time of day)
  • Tennis and padel: games for each side in each set; set format; No-Ad / Golden Point setting; match tiebreak setting; doubles setting
  • Pickleball: final score for each side; doubles setting; rally scoring setting; game target

If stored history ever becomes partly unreadable, the app keeps one copy of the unreadable data on the watch so it is not lost.

App preferences

  • Whether you have completed the welcome screen
  • Whether you have seen the gesture hints
  • Haptics: on or off

Backups

Backups of your devices, if you use them, are made by Apple's or Google's operating system under their own privacy policies. On Wear OS, CourtPoint excludes all of its data from Android backup and device-to-device transfer.

CourtPoint is a paid app on both platforms ($3.99, or the equivalent price in your local currency). There is no free trial and no in-app purchase.

  • Apple Watch: sold by Apple through the App Store before you install it, under Apple's terms and privacy policy (https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). For people in the EEA, Apple's privacy policy names Apple Distribution International Limited as the controller of this data.

The apps contain no purchase, billing or licence-check code. We never see or store your payment details, name, email address, Apple Account, Google Account or purchase history. Apple and Google give us sales reports, which we use only for accounting.

8. Your rights

Under the GDPR and UK GDPR

You have the right to access your data (Art. 15), to have it corrected (Art. 16), to have it erased (Art. 17), to restrict its processing (Art. 18), to data portability (Art. 20), to object to processing based on legitimate interest (Art. 21), and not to be subject to decisions based solely on automated processing (Art. 22). We make no such decisions.

Under KVKK (Turkey), Article 11

You have the right to:

  • learn whether your personal data is processed;
  • request information about it if it is;
  • learn the purpose of the processing and whether the data is used for that purpose;
  • know the third parties in Turkey or abroad to whom it is transferred;
  • request correction if it is incomplete or inaccurate;
  • request its deletion or destruction under the conditions in KVKK Article 7;
  • request that corrections, deletions or destructions be notified to the third parties to whom it was transferred;
  • object to a result against you that arises from analysis exclusively by automated systems;
  • claim compensation if you suffer damage because of unlawful processing.

Under other laws

If you live in a country whose law gives you other or additional rights over your personal data (for example Switzerland, Brazil, South Korea or a US state), contact us and we will handle your request under that law.

How to exercise them

Email enlightappscontact@gmail.com. We may ask you to confirm that the email address is yours. We answer free of charge, in writing by email, stating what we did or why we refused:

  • within one month under the GDPR (extendable by two months for complex requests, in which case we tell you within the first month);
  • within 30 days under KVKK (KVKK Art. 13). If a request needs extra cost, the fee set by the KVKK Board's tariff may apply.

Because the apps keep your match data only on your watch, we cannot access, export or delete it for you. You control it directly: delete history entries in the app, or delete the app.
