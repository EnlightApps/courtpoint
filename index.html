Privacy Policy

CourtPoint — Effective and last updated: October 23, 2026

This policy covers the CourtPoint app for Apple Watch and the CourtPoint website (all pages at https://enlightapps.github.io/courtpoint/, in English and Turkish). A Turkish translation is available; if the two versions differ, this English version prevails, except that for the KVKK information notice (aydınlatma metni) the Turkish text prevails.

CourtPoint is developed and sold by Aydın Akyol, an individual developer publishing under the name EnlightApps (EnlightApps is not a registered company). He is the controller (veri sorumlusu) for the personal data described in sections 6 and 12.

  • Contact for anything in this policy, including requests to exercise your rights: support@enlight-apps.com

2. Summary

  • The app keeps everything on your watch: scores, match times, match history and settings. None of it is sent to us or to anyone else. App settings can also be in your own device backups (section 3).
  • We have no server and no user accounts, and the app contains no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking code.
  • CourtPoint is a paid app, bought through the App Store before you install it. The app contains no purchase or billing code.
  • The personal data we receive comes from outside the app: emails and other messages you send us (section 6), and the reviews and crash reports Apple shows us (section 12).

3. Data stored on your watch

The app stores, on your watch: your match settings, so the next match starts the same way; the match in progress, saved after every point so it can resume and deleted when the match ends; the results of up to 200 finished matches, with scores and durations but no dates or times of day, each with a random identifier used only to select or delete it; and app preferences such as haptics. It uses the watch's standard on-device storage (UserDefaults). Other apps cannot access it, the app never sends it anywhere, and we never receive it. You can delete history entries in the app at any time, and everything is deleted from the watch when you delete the app.

Backups. Backups of your devices, if you use them, are made by Apple's operating system under Apple's privacy policy; we cannot access them. Apple states that the Apple Watch backup on your paired iPhone "includes app settings", and that when you back up your iPhone to iCloud or your computer, that backup includes your Apple Watch data (https://support.apple.com/en-us/108358). Copies of CourtPoint's settings, and possibly its other data, can therefore be in your own backups and stay there after you delete the app, until you delete the backup.

4. Purchase

CourtPoint is a paid app ($3.99, or the equivalent price in your local currency), sold through the App Store before you install it, under Apple's terms and privacy policy (https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). There is no free trial and no in-app purchase, and the app contains no purchase, billing or licence-check code. We never see your payment details or your Apple account. Apple gives us only aggregated sales reports (totals by country and currency), with nothing about individual buyers.

5. Website

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub states that "When a GitHub Pages site is visited, the visitor's IP address is logged and stored for security purposes" (https://docs.github.com/en/pages/getting-started-with-github-pages/about-github-pages). GitHub does this for its own purposes under the GitHub Privacy Statement (https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement); we do not receive or see these logs. Requests about them should go to GitHub's Data Protection Officer (dpo[at]github[dot]com).

The website sets no cookies and loads no analytics, fonts or scripts from other services. The PRIVACY POLICY screen in the app shows this page's address and can open it; GitHub then logs the IP address of the device that loads it. No other party collects personal information about your activity over time or across websites through our pages or app.

8. Your rights

Under KVKK (Turkey)

You have the rights in Article 11 of KVKK (https://www.mevzuat.gov.tr/MevzuatMetin/1.5.6698.pdf). How to use them is described below.

Under the GDPR

You have the right to access your data (Art. 15), to have it corrected (Art. 16), to have it erased (Art. 17), to restrict its processing (Art. 18), to data portability (Art. 20), to object to processing based on legitimate interest (Art. 21), and not to be subject to decisions based solely on automated processing (Art. 22). We make no such decisions.

Right to object

You can object at any time, on grounds relating to your situation, to processing based on our legitimate interests (sections 6 and 12). Email us and we will stop, unless we have compelling legitimate grounds that override your interests, rights and freedoms, or need the data to establish, exercise or defend legal claims. You can also ask us for the balancing test behind each legitimate interest.

How to exercise them

Email support@enlight-apps.com, in English or Turkish. We may ask you to confirm that the email address is yours. We answer free of charge, in writing by email, stating what we did, or why we refused and that you can complain to a data protection authority (section 9) or go to court:

  • within 30 days under KVKK (KVKK Art. 13(2));
  • within one month under the GDPR (extendable by two months for complex requests, in which case we tell you within the first month, with the reasons for the delay).

Because the app keeps your match data on your watch and never sends it to us, we cannot access, export or delete it for you. You control it directly: delete history entries in the app, or delete the app and any backups that hold it.

9. Complaints

You can complain to a data protection authority:

  • Turkey: Kişisel Verileri Koruma Kurumu (https://www.kvkk.gov.tr). Under KVKK you must apply to us first (KVKK Art. 14(2)); you can then complain to the Board within 30 days of learning our answer, and in any case within 60 days of applying (Art. 14(1)).
  • EU and EEA: a data protection authority, in particular in the EU/EEA country where you live or work, or where you think the GDPR was breached (list: https://www.edpb.europa.eu/about-edpb/about-edpb/members_en)

10. Children

CourtPoint is a sports scoring app for a general audience and is not directed at children. The app collects no personal data from anyone, including children. If we learn that a message comes from someone under 18, we reply once if needed, then promptly delete the message and the contact details instead of keeping them for the period in section 6. A parent or guardian can also ask us to delete such data, and we will.

11. Security

We keep the data we receive to the minimum described above, and only we can access our email account.

12. Ratings, reviews and crash reports

The "RATE COURTPOINT" button opens CourtPoint's page in the App Store. CourtPoint sends nothing when you tap it.

  • Reviews: if you rate or review CourtPoint, Apple shows us your rating, the review, the nickname you chose, the date and the App Store country, and lets us reply publicly on the store.
  • Crash reports: if you allow your device to share diagnostic and usage information with app developers, we may receive reports of CourtPoint crashes through Xcode. They contain the device model, system version, times of the crash and of the app launch, technical details of the crash, and an anonymised identifier for each device. CourtPoint itself contains no crash-reporting code.
